## What the scramble test does and does not test

- The sky-scramble null tests HD against **no spatial correlation**. It cannot separate HD from a uniform, monopole-like correlation. NANOGrav's isotropic scrambles share this property.
- The reason is the array's geometry. NG15's pulsars cluster on the sky, so Γ_HD's off-diagonal has mean 0.047, while an isotropic scramble's averages 7e-6.
- The pooled-mean G has off-diagonal mean 0.021, which contributes about 4.4 of S's 22.07.
- **HD with a free uniform term** (both the template and the scrambles mean-removed, `opening_readout.without_uniform`): S = 17.64, z = 5.58, 0 of 5000. K1 is unchanged.
  - This variant is conservative. It also discards HD's own projection onto the uniform direction: for this geometry an HD-only fit already predicts an off-diagonal mean of about 0.008–0.013.
- Review check (PR #139, not tracked code): a fit of G to HD with a free intercept leaves an intercept of 0.015 ± 0.012 under a pulsar jackknife. That is no significant evidence of a monopole.
- An HD-versus-monopole test belongs to a later multi-component check (Phase 3's simulations), not the K1 gate.

## Correctness gates (spec §3)

- **V1** (12 draws, 3 per chain): the contraction Σ G(Γ − I) against the forward-mode (JVP) ε-derivative through the harness's `get_likelihood`.
  - Strict per-draw relative error is 1.4e-11 for Γ_HD and 7.7e-11 for scramble 0. Gate 1e-6: pass.
  - `full.json` stores a floored version, max(|S|, median |S_HD| = 21.9) as the denominator, which gives 1.3e-11 and 4.4e-12.
  - A central difference (h = 1e-4) agrees to 3.2e-3 per draw for HD (0.065 absolute) and 1.2e-2 for the scramble (0.018 absolute).
    - The likely cause is filter rounding on lnL ≈ 7.8 × 10⁶ amplified by 1/h. That attribution is plausible but not shown on 67 pulsars.
    - JVP and reverse mode share one AD path, so the finite difference is the only independent reference.
    - The 3-pulsar test (`stat/test_orf_gradient.py`) gates it at ≤ 1e-4 and passes.
- **V2** (3 draws): lnL at C = I equals `SparseArrayFilter.get_likelihood` at ε = 0 with difference 0.0 nats. Gate 1e-8: pass.
  - The probe's stored lnL is matched to ≤ 1.8e-7 nats. This is the check that the draw → θ mapping is right.
- **Task 1.4 rerun:** 5 draws reproduce G to 7.7e-12 relative (4.4e-11 absolute).
  - The 5 ran as one batch of 5, whereas the 400-draw run used batches of 32.
  - This measures sensitivity to batch shape. It does not test same-batch determinism.
  - The `full.json` key is "atol", but the gate is applied to the relative difference.
- **Task 1.5:** per-draw S reproduces V1's contracted values to 2.7e-16 relative. This is not bitwise, because the BLAS reduction order differs with the matrix shape and the CPU. The clause says "exactly"; the gate used is ≤ 1e-12 relative.
